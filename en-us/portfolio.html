CNN for Recyclable Material

2025

Skills Learned

My College Completion Course (Trabalho de Conclusão de Curso - TCC) was a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify trash pictures into recyclable categories, encompassing Metal, Paper, Glass and Plastic. The group collected pictures in a Google Drive and used a Apps Script to update the ammount of pictures in each category, streamlining the data gathering process. Besides designing the CNN, we also developed an Android Application using a local AI model trained with Tensorflow to let the user take a picture and sort trash without need for a Wifi connection.
